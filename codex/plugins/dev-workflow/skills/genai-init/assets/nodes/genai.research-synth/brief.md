# Draw the conclusion, and say what to do about it

Two artifacts, and they are different documents:

- **`REPORT.mdx`** — what the evidence says. Every claim traceable to a finding. Carried as MDX and
  previewed through **`computer-use:mdx-artifact`**, because this is read by a person and content
  form ranks diagram > table > prose.
- **`PROPOSAL.md`** — what to do, and what it costs. One recommendation, the alternatives that were
  live, and what would have to be true for each to win.

**Answer the question that was asked.** Go back to it before writing. A report that answers a more
interesting adjacent question is the most common way this work is wasted.

**Where the findings are thin, say so in the conclusion**, not only in the findings. A reader who
reaches the recommendation should not have to go back two documents to learn it rests on one source.

If the evidence does not support a recommendation, that is the recommendation: say what would have
to be found out, and what it would cost to find out.
